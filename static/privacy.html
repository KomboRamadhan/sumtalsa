SumTalsa Privacy & Safeguarding

Collect only data needed for the educational purpose. Use learner codes where possible. Parents should only access linked learner records. Sensitive learner data should not be sent to AI services unless necessary, protected, and permitted by your school’s policy.

Production deployments should use HTTPS, strong secrets, role-based permissions, audit logs, backups, and a documented data-retention policy.

AI-generated content must be reviewed by a qualified teacher before high-stakes use.
